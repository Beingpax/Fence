Bento Improved

The parts you liked stay as they were: the big coral clock with "has your back", the greeting with New block, Pulls today and Up next. The coral tile now also notes the other block that's on. Timeline Notebook, made modern

The notebook's idea with Bento's type and colours. Each block gets a lane across the day: solid coral while it's on, faded once it's done, outlined when it's coming up later. A coral line with the time marks now, and each pull is a dot on its own lane. The coral clock and the greeting sit on top.

# Credits

## Pause wallpapers

The wallpapers in `Fence/Resources/Wallpapers` were generated for Fence. Acacia Plain,
Red Dune and Snowy Ridge used provided landscape references. Green Hills was generated
from a written brief. Each landscape has five matching lighting variants. The pause
rotates landscapes by local calendar day and chooses the lighting by local time.

Everything else in the app — the icon, the nudge character — is generated or drawn in
code. See `Tools/make-icon.swift` and `Fence/UI/MascotView.swift`.

## Adult domain catalog

The 18+ list's sites are a short, hand-picked list of the most visited 18+ sites. Its words are written for Fence. Several phrases come from the [List of Dirty, Naughty, Obscene, and Otherwise Bad Words](https://github.com/LDNOOBW/List-of-Dirty-Naughty-Obscene-and-Otherwise-Bad-Words), © 2012–2020 Shutterstock, Inc., licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
